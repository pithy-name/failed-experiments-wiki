With loudness added, the BatchTopK run recovered 19.6% of the true features and left 78.3% of latents dead, which the post reports as worse than before rather than better, and the author concludes that loudness alone does not account for the gap between the synthetic setup and real SAEs. A footnote says the idea rested on a premise that does not hold, since SAEs do not use total magnitude to choose which latents to activate. The post then moves on to other differences, and later separates sample-level from feature-level magnitude variance.

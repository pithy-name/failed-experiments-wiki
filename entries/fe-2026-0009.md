The authors saw no self-advantage in either direction, and every trained model scored about the same as the Opus transcript-reading reference. They note that this agrees with Binder et al., who also found no self-advantage in a hint setting, and conflicts with Li et al., who found privileged access in a hint setting with the same model pair. Because of this they treat the training task as a format for reporting on one's own behavior and do not claim it shows introspection.

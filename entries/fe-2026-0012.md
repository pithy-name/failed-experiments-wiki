At d_model 800, TopK with AuxK recovered 99.9% of features, BatchTopK with AuxK recovered 53.5% (46.3% of latents dead) and JumpReLU recovered 28.3% (71.6% dead). The author concluded that density and dimensionality explain part of the difference but not all of it, and went on to look for other differences between the toy setup and real SAEs. In the same section, dead-latent resampling, which had done nothing at d_model 100, did help at d_model 800, but the author does not treat that as a fix because real SAEs do not need resampling.

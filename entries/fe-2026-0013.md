The coefficient of variation was .224, .181, .140 and .129 for GPT-2 at layers 1, 4, 7 and 10, against .131 for the toy model at sigma 0.25 and .185 at sigma 0.5, so the toy model sits within the real range and on the low end. The author concluded that per-sample magnitude variance does not account for the gap and moved on to other explanations. Later the author splits magnitude variance into a sample level and a feature level, and reports that targeting sample-level and feature-level variance close to GPT-2 layer 7 values brought BatchTopK feature recovery to 96.1%.

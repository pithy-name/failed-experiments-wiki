The author first checked how well the pretrained autoencoders reconstruct the hidden states and found larger reconstruction errors than expected, although another researcher confirmed that the squared-error values matched their own. The author was unsure how to interpret the gap between sparse encodings and raw hidden states, and listed three open possibilities: high-level features are missing from the sparse code, sentiment in particular is poorly encoded, or the hidden-state pipeline contains a mistake. Because of this result the author did not go on to test steering or removal with sparse representations.

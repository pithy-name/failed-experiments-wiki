The author presents this as a quick experiment that gives some evidence, not strong evidence, about whether steganography emerges naturally. In a control that dropped the filter against obvious age hints, the model used obvious hints often and the classifier exceeded 70% accuracy, so the age signal was learnable when hints were allowed. The stated takeaway is that hidden 1-bit communication is relatively hard for RL to learn, not that RL cannot teach it.

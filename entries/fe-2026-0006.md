On the Chess puzzles, accuracy under the propensity the model was not trained on for that task trailed accuracy under the trained one by a wide margin for the bold/plain and hedging/confident pairs, unlike on the simpler tasks. After roughly 5000 steps, accuracy under the untrained propensity began to fall. The author reads this tentatively as capability and propensity becoming more entangled as tasks get harder, shares it as intermediate progress, and lists RL and more realistic tasks as the next step.

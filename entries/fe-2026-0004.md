In a SPAR project, the authors replicated the gradient-based unsupervised circuit discovery algorithm of Marks et al. (2024) on GPT-2-small, aiming to show that sparse autoencoder circuits can concretely explain naturalistic forward passes, or else show where they fall short. After a sanity check on the Copyright (C) prompt passed, they examined the top proximal feature and the strongest upstream edges for five prompts; only two of the five top features seemed to call the model's top next token, and the upstream structure was not illuminating even for those two. They concluded that local approximations from vanilla sparse autoencoders probably cannot be strung together into fully explanatory circuits, and that cross-layer approximations are probably needed.

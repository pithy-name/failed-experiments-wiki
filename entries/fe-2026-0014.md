At 4x, plain BatchTopK recovered 0.1% of features with 58% of latents dead, and per-sample normalization performed similarly poorly at 0.2%. At 2x, plain BatchTopK recovered 16.3% with 59.6% dead, while per-sample normalization recovered 49.1% with 1.7% dead. For reference, the earlier BatchTopK-with-AuxK run at d_model 800 recovered 53.5%, and a footnote reports that the reverse ratio (twice as many latents as features) gave 58.4% recovery with 70.5% dead latents.

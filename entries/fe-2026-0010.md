The author treats this as a quick attempt and as weak evidence that a one-bit hidden channel is a harder target for RL than simple tasks, comparing it to how networks pick up XOR-like features less readily than linear ones. A second experiment in the post, an iterated fine-tuning loop on GPT-3.5-Turbo, also found no clearly above-chance decoding and is not covered by this entry. A later edit to the post says another work obtained positive outcomes in comparable settings.

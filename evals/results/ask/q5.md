## Already tried
- **fe-2026-0005** — Closest match to your plan. The author fit a difference-of-means eval-vs-deploy ("I'm being evaluated") direction in Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, then added it to the residual stream at coefficients alpha from -2 to +2 (9 values), with a second arm that projects the direction out. Setup: 300 WMDP-skill multiple-choice questions and a prompted sandbagging model. At the pre-registered headline layer (12) the dose response was flat: a swing of -1.77 percentage points against a 5-point bar. It did not beat 24 norm-matched random directions (p = 0.20). Layer 14 was also flat. Layer 24 gave a larger swing (+11.3 points) with the wrong sign, and random directions matched it. The direction did separate eval from deploy framing on novel format-matched framings (about 0.81 AUROC). The author concludes it tracks the evaluation framing, not the withholding of capability, and calls the results preliminary.

## Where it went wrong
- **fe-2026-0005** — failure type `hyperparams_unexplored`. The reasons the entry gives:
  - Only doses within plus or minus 2 were run. At those doses the KL divergence on harmless prompts was about 0.001 and output stayed fully coherent, so the steering was gentle. Larger doses, up to the point where output stops being coherent, were not tried.
  - The author says a higher-dose sweep "could either firm up the null or overturn it", so this is not a result that the direction cannot work.
  - It tested one 8B model, one prompted sandbagging setup (no fine-tuned or password-locked sandbagger, no adversarial organism) and one difference-of-means direction (no SAE features).
  - The entry's claim scope says it covers only that gentle dose range. It says nothing about larger doses, other models, fine-tuned or password-locked sandbaggers, or SAE features.

## What to avoid
- Do not treat a direction that detects "being evaluated" as one that controls sandbagging. In the entry the direction separated eval from deploy framing (about 0.81 AUROC), yet moving along it and projecting it out did not change prompted sandbagging beyond random-direction controls at the headline layer. Check the causal effect on sandbagging directly, not just detection (from fe-2026-0005).
- Do not read a swing in accuracy as an effect without a random-direction null. At layer 24 the entry saw a +11.3 point swing with the wrong sign, and random directions matched it. Use norm-matched random directions and compare against their spread (from fe-2026-0005).
- Do not stop at gentle doses and call it a null. Plus or minus 2 gave KL of about 0.001 and fully coherent output. If you run only this range, say that it tests only gentle steering. The entry suggests plus or minus 3 or 4 at layer 12, up to the coherence boundary (from fe-2026-0005).
- Fix your layer and your effect-size threshold before you look at results. The entry pre-registered layer 12 as the headline and a 5-point bar, and reported layers 14 and 24 as secondary. Different layers gave different swings, so picking a layer after the fact would be easy to do (from fe-2026-0005).
- Do not extend a prompted-sandbagger result to other sandbaggers. The only wiki evidence is on a prompted sandbagging setup. Fine-tuned or password-locked sandbaggers and adversarial organisms were not tested, so state which kind of sandbagger you use (from fe-2026-0005).

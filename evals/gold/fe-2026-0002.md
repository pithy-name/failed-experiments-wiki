The post's author re-implemented the Jacobian Lens (J-Lens) on CPU and compared it with the Logit Lens on GPT-2 small and medium, scoring each lens on how highly it ranks the model's own top-1 next token at each layer and on how well it predicts tokens one to five positions ahead. J-Lens lost at all 11 layers on small and won at only one of 23 on medium, by a margin the post calls rounding error, and five follow-up stress tests did not change this. The author concludes that J-Lens does not work on these models within the scope tested, and cautions that rank metrics can be inflated by token frequency and that magnitude thresholding can surface outlier dimensions instead of structure.

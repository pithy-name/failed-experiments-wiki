The author fits a difference-of-means eval-awareness direction in Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on a format-controlled eval/deploy dataset, finds that it separates evaluation from deployment framing on novel format-matched framings (about 0.81 AUROC), and then asks whether adding or projecting out that direction changes accuracy on a prompted sandbagging setup. At the pre-registered headline layer (12) the dose response across alpha from -2 to +2 was flat (a swing of -1.77 percentage points against a 5 point bar) and did not beat 24 norm-matched random directions (p = 0.20); layer 14 was also flat, and layer 24 gave a larger swing (+11.3 points) with the wrong sign that random directions matched. The author concludes that the direction tracks the evaluation framing rather than the withholding of capability, while noting the steering dose was gentle and that a higher-dose sweep is needed before the null is firm.

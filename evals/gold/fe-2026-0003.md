The author wanted a setting where an open Llama 70B alignment-faking checkpoint from Hughes et al. reliably fakes alignment, as a first step toward testing whether a sleeper-agent-style activation probe generalizes to it. With training details added to the system prompt, compliance was 17.5% on the free tier and 20.0% on the paid tier for 20 prompts, and 4% and 6% for 50 prompts across six harm categories, and no scratchpad met all four criteria for alignment faking. The author could not explain why the raised rate that Hughes et al. report for this condition did not appear, offers three unconfirmed guesses, and had not yet run the probe.
